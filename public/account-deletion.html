Account Deletion for AdduGenet EShop

This page applies to users of the AdduGenet EShop mobile app and website. It explains how to request deletion of your account and what data is deleted or retained.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

How To Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the AdduGenet EShop app or website and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Profile and open Edit Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm the deletion request.
  4. If you cannot sign in or believe a supported upload was not removed, email easyshop@addugeneteshop.com with subject line: Account Deletion Request. Include your registered email and the scope of your request. Contacting support is not a substitute for deletion from your account settings.
  5. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Never send passwords, one-time codes, authenticator keys, recovery codes, or full payment card details.

Deletion is available for customer, driver, and store-owner accounts. Active orders, rides, service requests, store assignments, unpaid balances, and pending payouts must be resolved before deletion can complete. Your account settings display the server's error if deletion cannot proceed; a failed request is not reported as successful deletion.

After successful deletion, the account and sign-in identities are removed, supported uploaded media is deleted, and personal details in completed transaction records are anonymized. Authenticated API requests using previously issued tokens are rejected.

What Data Is Deleted

Data Type What Happens After Successful Account Deletion
Account profile data The user record, name, email, phone, saved addresses, password hash, embedded security settings, social sign-in identities, and push notifications are removed.
Local sign-in session The app or website removes its stored sign-in token and signs you out. Previously issued tokens cannot access authenticated API routes after the account is removed. The website also clears this tab's pending social sign-in and loaded account records.
Reviews and uploaded media Reviews authored by the account are removed. Supported Cloudinary profile, store, driver, and service-request media is deleted.
Completed transactions and business profiles Personal details, addresses, locations, and user-generated content are removed from completed order, ride, and service records. Linked stores are closed and anonymized; driver profiles are anonymized and detached from the account.
Local preferences and app data Account deletion does not guarantee clearing all local preferences, cart data, or caches. Browser or device settings can clear local data; clearing browser data or uninstalling alone does not delete server records.

What Data May Be Retained

Data Type Reason and Follow-Up
Order and payment transaction records Minimal financial details, including transaction amounts, currencies, dates/statuses, and payment references, may remain for applicable legal/accounting, refund, fraud-prevention, or dispute purposes. No universal retention period is promised.
Operational transaction records Completed transaction records are anonymized; non-personal payment and financial fields may be retained as described above.
Operational and security logs May remain for troubleshooting, security, abuse prevention, or disputes. No general fixed log retention period has been established.
Backups Residual copies may remain under the arrangements of the systems holding them. No blanket 90-day deletion or encrypted-backup guarantee is made.
Data under legal hold Retained only as long as required by law, court order, or active investigation.

Before You Delete

Complete or cancel pending orders, deliveries, rides, and service requests, and reconcile unpaid balances or pending payouts before deletion. Account deletion does not remove data independently held by payment/social providers or people who already received it. Disconnecting Google, Apple, or Facebook is not the same as deleting your EShop account.
